# Privacy & GRC Portfolio

**GDPR · Data Protection · Governance, Risk & Compliance**

Welcome to my professional portfolio of practical privacy and information security governance projects.

This repository documents how I approach compliance assignments: researching applicable requirements, defining scope, identifying information gaps, and preparing structured, traceable documentation.

The projects are developed as portfolio exercises using fictional organisations and scenarios. They are not representations of commissioned client work or formally approved compliance assessments.

## Projects

### 01 — Nordcenter ApS: CCTV Data Protection Impact Assessment

**Status: Work in Progress**

A GDPR DPIA exercise for a fictional Danish shopping centre operator planning to deploy 120 CCTV cameras in publicly accessible common areas, with approximately 80,000 visitors per week.

The project covers the basis for conducting a DPIA under Article 35 GDPR, the proposed processing activities, data flows, actors, technical dependencies, scope boundaries, and outstanding questions requiring validation.

The main DPIA is currently preliminary: the published document covers its initial sections, while the scope is documented separately. Necessity and proportionality, risks, safeguards, and residual risk assessment remain to be completed.

**[Explore the Nordcenter CCTV DPIA project](./Nordcenter-CCTV-DPIA/)**

The project includes a methodology and approach document, fictional company case, preliminary DPIA, and supporting DPIA scope document.

## Approach and transparency

- **Evidence-based documentation:** Distinguishing case facts, applicable requirements and unresolved questions.
- **Clear scope:** Defining what is included, excluded or still requires confirmation.
- **Work in progress:** Clearly identifying unfinished analysis rather than presenting preliminary work as final.
- **AI transparency:** Where AI assists with fictional scenarios, drafting or review, that involvement is documented in the relevant project's methodology.
- **No invented validation:** Stakeholder input, sign-offs and confirmations are not represented as having occurred unless actually performed.

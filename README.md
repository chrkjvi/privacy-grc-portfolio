# Privacy & GRC Portfolio

**GDPR · Data Protection · Privacy Operations · Governance, Risk & Compliance**

Welcome to my portfolio of independent, practical privacy and compliance projects.

I use fictional cases to demonstrate how I research regulatory requirements, assess facts and information gaps, make reasoned decisions, and translate those decisions into structured documentation and operational workflows. These are portfolio exercises, not commissioned client work or formally validated assessments.

## Projects

### 01 — Nordcenter ApS: CCTV Data Protection Impact Assessment

**Status: Work in Progress**

A preliminary GDPR DPIA exercise concerning a fictional Danish shopping centre and a proposed CCTV system.

The project focuses on the legal basis for conducting a DPIA under Article 35, the proposed processing activities, data flows, stakeholders, technical dependencies, scope boundaries, and questions that require validation. The full assessment of necessity, proportionality, risks, safeguards, and residual risks remains outstanding.

### 02 — Article 15 DSAR: EU Representative Perspective

**Status: Portfolio assessment completed; proposed workflow not executed**

A fictional data subject access request received by an EU representative on behalf of **Northstar Outfitters LLC**, a US e-commerce controller. The exercise examines how an EU representative could receive, register, classify, forward, track, and coordinate an Article 15 request, while the controller retains responsibility for responding.

The project includes a 12-stage **Operational Assessment & Decision Record** setting out issues, case-specific assessments, decisions, legal references, and proposed actions, plus a structured Excel case tracker. Hypothetical follow-up events are labelled; no actual client correspondence or case closure occurred.

Documents: fictional case study, assessment and decision record, and case tracker.

## Approach and transparency

- **Research and reasoning:** Distinguishing applicable legal requirements from suggested operational practice.
- **Case-based analysis:** Assessing known facts while identifying uncertainties and assumptions.
- **Traceability:** Showing the rationale for proposed decisions, next actions, and responsibilities.
- **Clear project status:** Distinguishing preliminary work, completed analysis exercises, and actions not actually performed.
- **AI transparency:** AI tools have supported fictional scenario development, drafting, editing, and review. The relevant project materials explain the limitations; the portfolio should not be treated as independent legal validation.
- **No invented validation:** Stakeholder interviews, confirmations, approvals, and live case handling are not presented as having occurred.
